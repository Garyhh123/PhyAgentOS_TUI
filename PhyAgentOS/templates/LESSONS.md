## Lesson Format

When writing a lesson, use this format:

```
### Lesson: [what you tried to do]

- **Task**: brief description of the goal
- **Strategy**: what approach you took (action types, params, reasoning)
- **Outcome**: success or failure, with specific evidence from ENVIRONMENT.md
- **Insight**: what you learned, and what to try differently next time
- **Escalation**: if this approach failed 2+ times, what simpler alternative to use
```
